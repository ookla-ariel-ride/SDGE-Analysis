Purpose. This report is a decision document for one household: given a full year of measured energy data, what should this home do about its rate plan, its behavior, storage, and its solar array? Every conclusion is computed from data by committed scripts; nothing is asserted from intuition or vendor claims. It works to answer these questions:
  1. Am I on the best electric rate plan? (§3)
  2. Does adding a battery change which plan is best? (§4)
  3. What behavior changes are worth real money? (§5, §9, §13)
  4. Does a home battery pay — which one, dispatched how, honest payback? (§6, §7)
  5. Should I add/upgrade panels or microinverters? (§8)
  6. Should I electrify gas appliances? (§10)
  7. Has the solar system paid for itself? (§11)
  8. Is panel cleaning worth it — cadence and month? (§12)
  9. When is grid power cheapest and cleanest — do they align? (§13)
  10. What is NEM grandfathering worth per year? (§13)
Every answer carries its evidence: measured, modeled, or estimated (§14 legend). Absolute dollars anchor to real bills; projections are labeled with their rate basis.
