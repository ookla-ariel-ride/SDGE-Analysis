Questions this report examines: plans, behavior, and hardware

  1. Am I on the best electric rate plan? Answered for the tested plans and year. The separate CEA-versus-bundled generation comparison remains conditional.
  2. Does adding a battery change which plan is best? Answered within the matched plan model.
  3. What behavior changes are worth real money? Conditional modeled savings. Charging, cooling, and other load studies have separate limits; overlapping savings cannot be added.
  4. Does a home battery pay for itself: which one, dispatched how, at what payback? Conditional on cost and controls. Controller value, program revenue, and backup use separate assumptions.
  5. Should I add panels, install higher-capacity panels, or upgrade microinverters? Defer conventional expansion. Remaining headroom, component faults, and annual clipping loss remain not determined.

Questions this report examines: electrification, solar value, and timing

  1. Should I electrify gas appliances? Conditional cases; appliance shares, schedules, electrical fit, and gas-account closure remain unresolved.
  2. Has the solar system paid for itself, and what is it worth per year now? Conditional lifetime model; historical household use and the ITC claim are not established.
  3. Was paying for panel cleaning worth it, and what cadence and month are optimal? Production response estimated. Dollar recovery and optimal cadence remain not determined.
  4. When is grid power cheapest and cleanest, and do those windows align? Answered for tariff windows and average grid emissions. Marginal emissions remain uncomputed.
  5. What is NEM 2.0 grandfathering actually worth per year? Conditional current-rate comparison. The best purchase date around NEM expiry remains not determined.

Balcony systems and open studies

  • What do balcony panels and portable storage add, with or without a proposed home battery? Conditional model; panel-only, storage-only, and joint comparisons use matched starting cases.
  • Which equipment and complete cost can those savings support? Conditional cost ceilings. Complete quotes, supported connections, and exact system certification remain unknown.
  • Does a qualifying receptacle-connected PV system preserve NEM treatment, and what changes at the statutory sunset? Approval relief has conditions; export compensation and future account treatment remain not determined.
  • Is the service voltage within specification, and does it reduce solar production or cost money? Sampled evidence exists. Formal compliance and causal energy or dollar loss remain not determined.
  • Can this home's data reliably identify individual appliances? Open study. Accuracy untested; independent controlled-load records and scored household results remain missing.

Why do modeled bills differ from actual bills? The report explains part of the gap; the remainder stays unresolved. Fixed charges remain after improvements, while non-bypassable charges depend on physical flows.

They need onsite-consumption intent, current electrical-code compliance, nationally recognized laboratory system certification, and certified outage isolation. A component listing alone does not certify the complete system. The chaptered text grants approval relief and permits simple online notice; notice cannot require approval.

The official text endpoint returned an access error during the source check. The disclosed chaptered-text mirror, sections 8530–8532 supplies these details. The text establishes neither automatic shutdown nor permanent exemption. Applicable future equipment and account treatment, and any compliance cost, remain unknown.
